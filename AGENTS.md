# Agent rules for pqc-chain-budget

- `PRIOR-ART.md` governs every claim that leaves this repo. The words "first",
  "only", and "no one has" are banned in public artifacts. The permitted claim
  wording is in that file, verbatim; do not paraphrase it stronger.
- Every figure carries a stamp: Verified (measured here, script and output ship
  with it), Reported (cited to a source read in full), or Proposed (projected
  under stated assumptions). All PQ projections are Proposed, permanently.
- Load-bearing citations are verified by downloading and grepping the full text.
  Never trust a search summary; this project family has logged five
  fabricated or misattributed summaries to date.
- `.sources/` stays gitignored. No third-party full texts ever ship in this
  repo, public or private.
- The ePrint 2026/866 publication gate is CLOSED (read in full 2026-08-07, does
  not preempt; see PRIOR-ART.md). Re-sweep prior art before any NEW public claim.
- Prose follows `~/.rocky/steering/writing-style.md`. No em dashes, no banned
  vocabulary, sentence-case headings.
- No AI co-author trailers in commits.
